About this page. An interactive companion to a Power BI star-schema dashboard built on the METABRIC clinical cohort. Every figure is recalculated in your browser from the 2,509 de-identified patient records. Death rate is all-cause and uses patients with known survival status (1,981 overall) as the denominator. The grey bars show the trap a plain MEDIAN() falls into: patients still alive count their follow-up as if it were survival. Descriptive, exploratory analysis only. Associations here do not establish causation, treatment effectiveness or individual prognosis. Source: METABRIC via cBioPortal (study brca_metabric).
